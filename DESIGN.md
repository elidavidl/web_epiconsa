## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an institutional, sacred, warm, and highly credible tone tailored for the official distribution entity of the Episcopal Conference of Peru. The audience encompasses dioceses, parishes, religious communities, academic institutions, and faithful laypeople seeking liturgical supplies, official texts, devotional items, and religious literature.

The aesthetic philosophy balances classical ecclesiastical heritage with modern institutional commerce:
- **Tone:** Reverent, authoritative, welcoming, orderly, and transparent.
- **Style Direction:** Modern Institutional Classicism. It utilizes generous ivory breathing room, dignified serif display typography, deliberate gold accents, and deep navy grounding surfaces.
- **Interface Balance:** Structured functional commerce combined with elevated institutional poise. Dense product layouts are softened by gentle rounded boundaries, subtle warm ambient lighting, and accessible touch ergonomics.

## Colors

The palette establishes hierarchical gravity, warmth, and explicit contextual function:

- **Primary (`#1F2A4D`)**: Deep Navy symbolizes pastoral authority, structural solidity, and institutionality. Applied to primary action buttons, key brand banners, top navigation anchors, and dominant headings.
- **Secondary (`#C9A24B`)**: Soft Gold evokes dignity, sacred quality, and distinction. Used sparingly for badge accents, star ratings, primary hover states, active indicators, and special promotional badges.
- **Tertiary (`#5B2A86`)**: Liturgical Purple reflects sacred liturgical seasons (Lent, Advent, penitential dates) and solemn institutional notices. It is restricted to seasonal notification banners, liturgical book badges, and ecclesiastical campaign ribbons.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6EE`)**: Warm Ivory/Cream forms the global canvas backdrop, avoiding clinical modern stark white and providing a warm, bookish, parchment-like foundation.
- **Named Color (`#25D366`)**: WhatsApp Green is strictly isolated to customer service triggers, order assistance links, and direct WhatsApp checkout queries. It must never be applied to generic interface elements or unrelated success notifications.

## Typography

The type system blends the traditional solemnity of liturgical manuscripts with digital legibility:
- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the intellectual and historical gravity of the Episcopal Conference. Used for main hero messaging, category section headers, featured catalog highlights, and institutional decrees.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Maximizes readability in long pastoral documents, book descriptions, multi-tiered price catalogs, and checkout inputs.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize slightly tightened tracking for cohesion, while all-caps labels (SKUs, metadata tags, badge labels) use open letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8px base rhythm within a responsive 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of `1280px`:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer container margin `margin-desktop: 3rem`. Product catalogs render in 4-column or 3-column configurations with comfortable vertical air between shelves.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, container margin `2rem`. E-commerce filters shift into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, margin `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Product listings reflow to a 2-column compact grid or stacked single cards.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Institutional clarity requires breathing space. Use `space-xl` and `space-2xl` around promotional banners and catalog section titles, reserving tighter increments (`space-xs` to `space-md`) strictly for internal card layouts and form groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the sober and dignified character of the institution, avoid loud drop shadows or glowing glass effects. Depth is articulated through subtle warm tonal layering and soft ambient contact shadows:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Tone `#FAF6EE` (Warm Ivory).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Modules):** Tone `#FFFFFF` with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #E8E1D5`).
- **Elevation Level 1 (Static Cards & Input Fields):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 42, 77, 0.04)`.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(31, 42, 77, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(31, 42, 77, 0.03)`.
- **Elevation Level 3 (Sticky Header, Cart Bar, Modals):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(31, 42, 77, 0.12)`.
- **Liturgical Seasonal Bars:** Flush, solid tertiary fill `#5B2A86` with zero elevation to maintain formal alignment across the browser boundary.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), honoring formal institutional architecture:

- **Cards, Modals, Banners:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius for subtle softness without appearing childish or overly casual.
- **Form Controls & Standard Buttons:** `0.25rem` to `0.375rem` radius providing defined, clean boundaries that instill confidence during financial transactions.
- **WhatsApp Floating Button & Badges:** Circular / pill shapes (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for floating communication widgets, notification counters, and category pills to stand apart from structural content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Navy background (`#1F2A4D`), text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Minimum height: `44px` for touch accessibility. Hover: `#2A3A6B`.
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Gold background (`#C9A24B`), text `#1F2A4D`, semi-bold. Used for "Comprar Ahora" or primary conversion milestones. Hover: `#B38F3D`.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Transparent fill with `1px solid #1F2A4D`, text `#1F2A4D`. Hover: `#FAF6EE`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Solid WhatsApp green (`#25D366`), text `#FFFFFF` with WhatsApp icon preceding text. Exclusively for direct sales assistance and advisory. Hover: `#20BA5A`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E8E1D5`, `0.5rem` border radius.
- **Image Area:** Neutral background padding with image zoom of 1.03 on hover.
- **Content:** Playfair Display for product title, Inter medium for liturgical category tag, prominent bold navy price, and gold accent for liturgical/ecclesiastical badges (e.g., "Aprobado CEP", "Tiempo de Cuaresma").
- **Card Hover:** Subtle elevation step (`Level 2`) and hairline border color shift to `#C9A24B`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5CDBD`, text `#1F2A4D`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, rounded `0.25rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#1F2A4D`, outer ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(31, 42, 77, 0.15)`, outline none.
- **Error State:** Border color `#B3261E`, with error microcopy in `12px Inter`.

### Liturgical Banners & Seasonal Alerts
- **Appearance:** Full-width strip using `#5B2A86` with clear white text (`#FFFFFF`) and an optional gold link (`#C9A24B`). Used strictly during liturgical transitions (Adviento, Cuaresma, Pascua) or national episcopal communiqués.

### Chips & Category Filters
- **Default:** Background `#FAF6EE`, border `1px solid #E8E1D5`, text `#1F2A4D`, font size `13px`.
- **Selected:** Background `#1F2A4D`, border `1px solid #1F2A4D`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style:** Square with `2px` radius (checkbox) and circular (radio). Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #8C92A4`. Checked: background `#1F2A4D` with crisp white checkmark/dot. Accessible tap boundary of at least `32x32px`.
